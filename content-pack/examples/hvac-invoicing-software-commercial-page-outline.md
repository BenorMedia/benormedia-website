## **HVAC Invoicing Software FAQs**

### **Can I take a deposit before an install and charge the balance after?**

Yes. Use Scheduled Payments to collect a deposit now or on a future date. Then charge the balance when the job's done, using the card on file.

### **Do I have to buy a card reader?**

No. SwipeSimple allows multiple ways to pay without a reader: Tap to Pay on iPhone, manual keyed entry, invoice text-to-pay and invoice emails, payment links, etc. can all be done using your phone.

### **Will my shop get approved?**

Every merchant goes through an underwriting review before approval, and approval is never automatic. SwipeSimple's US-based team is available by phone and email any time you have a question.

### **Can a customer pay me later from their phone?**

Yes. Send an invoice by email, or use [Text to Pay](https://home.swipesimple.com/text-to-pay) to text them a secure link. They pay in a few taps.

### **Can I bill customers on a maintenance agreement?**

Yes. Scheduled Payments can charge a saved card on a recurring schedule, so plan billing runs on its own.

### **Does it work with QuickBooks?**

Yes. QuickBooks Online Sync connects your payments to your books.
