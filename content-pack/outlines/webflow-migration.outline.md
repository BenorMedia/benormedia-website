## **When staying on WordPress is the better call**

Not every WordPress site should move. Look hard at these cases before you commit:

- **A very large content library.** Compare your item counts with Webflow's current CMS limits first.
- **An online store on WooCommerce.** Confirm that Webflow's e-commerce features fit your catalog, checkout, and tax rules.
- **Member-only content.** Work out how logins and gated pages would work after the move.
- **Plugins that carry custom logic.** Decide what replaces each one before you commit.
- **Complex multi-language setups.** Confirm how language versions, URLs, and hreflang tags carry over.

If the site already works for your team and none of these cases fits, staying on WordPress is a fair answer. [Webflow vs WordPress](https://www.benormedia.com/guides/webflow-vs-wordpress) compares the two platforms.

## **WordPress to Webflow migration FAQs**

### **Can I keep my WordPress URLs when I move to Webflow?**

Sometimes. Static pages can keep their paths, but any URL that changes needs a 301 redirect to its new address. The redirect map we build before the build starts shows which URLs change.

### **Do I need 301 redirects when I migrate from WordPress to Webflow?**

Yes, for every URL that changes. A 301 is a permanent redirect, which Google recommends for site moves. Webflow accepts redirects one at a time or in bulk from a CSV file.

### **Will my site go offline during the migration?**

A planned migration does not need downtime. Your WordPress site can stay live while the Webflow site is built and tested on a staging copy, and the switch happens when the domain is pointed at the new site.

### **How long does a WordPress to Webflow migration take?**

It depends on the number of templates, the volume of CMS content, the redirects to map, the integrations, and how many review rounds your team needs. The timeline is set in the website strategy plan, once the audit shows how much there is to move.

### **Will I lose my rankings when I migrate?**

Rankings can move for a while after any migration, and Google says to expect temporary fluctuation. What costs rankings is an unplanned move, which is why every URL is mapped, every redirect is tested, and the site is monitored after launch.

### **Can you migrate my blog posts automatically?**

Largely, yes. Webflow documents a route that exports WordPress content as XML, converts it to CSV, and imports it into a CMS Collection. Each imported post still needs a check for formatting, images, internal links, and SEO fields.

### **How much does a WordPress to Webflow migration cost?**

It depends on the size and scope of the site: pages, templates, CMS content, integrations, and whether the design changes. The pricing page lists the Design + Development and One Off plans. Send the page count and the tools your site uses to get a number.

### **Do you migrate sites from other platforms?**

Webflow's partner directory lists platform migrations among BenorMedia's services. The method is the same for any move: map the URLs, move the content, redirect what changes, test, launch, and monitor. Tell us which platform your site runs on.
