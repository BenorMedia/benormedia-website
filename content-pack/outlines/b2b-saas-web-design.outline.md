## **When another option is better**

This page is about the public marketing website of a B2B SaaS company. Another option is better in these cases:

- The site is one page, and a template or a page builder is enough.
- The work is the product interface, such as in-app screens or a logged-in dashboard.
- The positioning is undecided, and a new design cannot settle who the product is for.
- Nobody on your team will own the site after launch.
- Engineering requires the marketing site to live inside the product codebase.

## **B2B SaaS web design FAQs**

### **What is B2B SaaS web design?**

It is the design and build of the marketing website for a business software company. The site explains the product, shows proof, answers pricing and security questions, and moves a buyer toward a demo or trial. It covers the public website, not the product interface.

### **Can my marketing team update the site without a developer?**

Within limits, yes. We build a component library and a CMS structure so your team can edit content and assemble pages from approved pieces. Changing the design system or creating new CMS Collections still calls for a designer or developer. Live training and documentation come at handoff.

### **How long does a B2B SaaS website project take?**

It depends on the number of page templates, the volume of CMS content, the integrations, whether the design is new or supplied, and the number of review rounds. The timeline is set in the website strategy plan, before the build starts.

### **How much does a B2B SaaS website cost?**

The same five drivers set the cost. The pricing page lists a Design + Development plan with unlimited design and development requests, billed monthly, and a One Off plan for a fixed scope. Send your page count and the tools the site must connect to.

### **Do you handle SEO and AI search?**

Yes. Technical SEO, schema, semantic structure and answer engine optimization are built into every site we launch, and the Growth service covers ongoing SEO and AI search work after launch. No agency controls whether a search engine or an AI assistant shows a given page.

### **Should a SaaS company redesign its website or refresh it?**

Refresh when the structure still matches how buyers decide and the message is out of date. Redesign when the structure no longer fits. In both cases, list the URLs that earn traffic first, and give each URL that changes a permanent redirect.

### **Can you support the site after launch?**

Yes. Ongoing website support covers weekly updates, quarterly CRO audits, technical SEO monitoring, and bug fixes and maintenance, with SLA-backed response times from a 48-hour standard response to 6-hour critical bug fixes.
