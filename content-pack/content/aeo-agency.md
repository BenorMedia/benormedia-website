An AEO agency, or GEO agency, works toward one outcome: your company named and cited when buyers ask AI assistants what to buy. We track the prompts your buyers use in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google's AI answers. Then we fix the pages, technical gaps and third-party mentions that decide if you show up.

AI visibility tracking and reporting are part of every Growth engagement, on Webflow and custom-coded sites.

## Why AI search now shapes B2B software shortlists.

Many software buyers now ask AI assistants before they turn to traditional search engines, and the answer can decide who makes the shortlist. Four numbers from the last eighteen months show where buyer search behavior stands.

- **Research starts in chat.** In [G2's April 2026 survey](https://company.g2.com/news/g2-research-the-answer-economy), 51% of B2B software buyers said they start research with an AI chatbot more often than with Google.
- **AI is in nearly every purchase.** [Forrester's State of Business Buying 2026](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/state-of-business-buying-2026/) found 94% of business buyers use AI during their buying process.
- **AI names vendors.** In [G2's 2026 Buyer Behavior Report](https://company.g2.com/news/buyer-behavior-2026), 82% of buyers had received software recommendations from an AI chatbot in the previous two years.
- **Fewer clicks reach your site.** When Google showed an AI summary, [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) found people clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits, against 15% when there was no summary.

## What AEO and GEO actually involve.

Answer engine optimization (AEO) and generative engine optimization (GEO) are two names for the same job: making your company the one AI systems mention and link to. Some agencies sell it as AI SEO or AI search optimization. Google says its AI features have [no special requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) beyond being indexed and eligible for a snippet. So the work stands on traditional SEO and adds four things most SEO programs skip.

- **Prompt tracking.** A fixed set of buyer questions, run across AI platforms on a schedule, because answers change from one run to the next. Prompts are longer and more specific than keywords, like "best SOC 2 automation tool for a 50-person fintech", and that's where buyers ready to shortlist show up.
- **Answer-shaped pages.** Pages that give direct answers, with the numbers and the trade-offs in plain sentences AI models can quote.
- **Third-party presence.** Brand mentions on the lists, review sites and communities AI systems cite when your own site isn't enough.
- **Entity and knowledge graph optimization.** The same company name, description and category on your site, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, G2 and Wikidata where you qualify, tied together with Organization schema and sameAs links.

If your site runs on Webflow, our [Webflow SEO and AEO](page:webflow-seo-agency) page covers the platform settings that matter.

## How we run an AEO and GEO program.

Our generative engine optimization services follow the same six steps every time. The first two set your AEO strategy, and they finish before we change anything on your site.

- **1. Prompt set.** 30 to 50 questions your buyers ask, built from sales calls, keyword research and search data: category, comparison, alternatives, problem and pricing prompts.
- **2. Baseline.** How often each AI platform names you, which competitors it names instead, and which pages it cites as sources. We also review your wider digital presence: reviews, listings and mentions.
- **3. Technical optimization.** robots.txt rules for search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Claude-SearchBot, content rendered on the server, and Bing Webmaster Tools with IndexNow for Copilot and Bing.
- **4. Pages that answer.** New and rewritten pages for the prompts you lose: comparisons, alternatives, use cases, pricing, integrations and FAQs. We plan them in topic clusters, so the content strategy builds topical authority instead of one-off posts.
- **5. Digital PR and mentions.** Outreach to the lists and publications AI already cites in your category, link building where it's earned, and review and community work.
- **6. Reporting.** Mention rate by platform and prompt, cited pages, AI-referred visits and the pipeline they bring in, every month.

## What the evidence says gets cited.

A lot of what's sold as AEO is guesswork. These are the findings we work from, with their sources.

- **Statistics, quotations and sources.** In the [research paper that coined GEO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) (KDD 2024), which tested generative search engines, adding citations, quotations and statistics raised visibility in AI answers by up to 40% in the authors' benchmark. Keyword stuffing didn't help.
- **Lists and third-party pages.** In an [Ahrefs study of 750 prompts](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/), "best of" lists made up 43.8% of the page types ChatGPT cited. In our own tracking for the web agency category, roundups were 25 of the 40 pages AI assistants cited most. [VERIFY #AEO-1: OK to publish our own tracking data | default: DELETE-LINE]
- **llms.txt isn't a lever yet.** Google says its Search ignores the file, and an [SE Ranking analysis of about 300,000 domains](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/llms-txt-shows-no-clear-effect-on-ai-citations-based-on-300k-domains/561542/) found no measurable effect on AI citations. We add one, but we don't sell it as strategy.
- **Schema supports, it doesn't switch anything on.** Google says [structured data isn't required](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) for generative AI search. We use it where it describes the page accurately.

## How we measure AI search visibility.

AI answers vary between runs, so one screenshot proves nothing. Our AI search visibility tracking measures trends across a fixed set of prompts, because unlike traditional SEO there's no single rank position to report.

- **Mention rate.** The share of tracked prompts and runs where each platform names you, broken down by platform and prompt type.
- **Citations.** AI citation frequency: which of your pages AI answers link to, and how often. We add the citation data [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview) reports for Copilot and Bing's AI summaries.
- **AI-referred traffic and pipeline.** Visits from ChatGPT, which tags its links with utm_source=chatgpt.com, and from Perplexity, Gemini and others, followed through to demo requests.
- **Google's AI features.** AI Overview visibility from the Search Console reports Google added in 2026, which cover AI Overviews and AI Mode, next to your Google rankings for the same topics.

## When AEO shouldn't be your priority.

AEO pays off over months, and it depends on foundations. Put it later on the list if one of these fits.

- **Google can't index your site properly.** Fix technical SEO first. Google's AI features draw on the same index, so search visibility problems carry straight over.
- **Nobody searches for your category yet.** In a new category, PR and brand building create the brand authority and mentions AEO needs.
- **You need pipeline this month.** AEO compounds slowly. Paid search and outbound are faster.
- **You have no proof to publish.** Without case studies, numbers or named customers, there's little for AI systems to cite.
- **You want a guarantee.** No one controls what AI platforms say. Anyone promising placement is guessing or gaming the system.
