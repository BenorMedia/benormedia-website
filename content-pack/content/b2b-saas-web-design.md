## The pages a SaaS buyer expects

In a March 2026 survey of 646 B2B buyers, [Gartner found that 67% prefer a rep-free experience](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-09-gartner-sales-survey-finds-67-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-experience), so the site has to answer their questions without a salesperson.

- **Home and product pages.** Who the product is for, what it does, and one clear next step.
- **Pricing.** Enough to judge fit and budget before a call.
- **Integrations.** Proof that the product works with the buyer's tools.
- **Customers and proof.** Named customers and approved quotes.
- **Security and trust.** Answers to the questions IT and procurement ask.
- **Demo or contact.** A short form that reaches your CRM.

Our guide to [B2B SaaS website pages](page:b2b-saas-website-pages) covers each one in detail.

## A component library your marketers can build from

We build a component system first and pages come out of it, so your team assembles new layouts from pieces that already match.

- Reusable components, variables, and design tokens.
- Standardized patterns for the pages a SaaS site repeats, such as feature, integration, and use-case pages.
- CMS architecture for resources, customer stories, and integration pages.
- Designs implemented to the pixel, with interactions, breakpoints, and the edge cases nobody briefs.
- Campaign landing pages put together from approved pieces, without booking a developer.

## SEO and AI search built into the site

SEO and AI search belong in the build and not in a later project, so every site is prepared for both traditional search and AI-first discovery.

- **Technical SEO and schema.** The technical foundation that search engines and AI systems read.
- **Semantic structure and topical authority.** Page structure and content planned around the topics your buyers search.
- **Answer engine optimization (AEO).** Content that AI assistants can understand, cite, and recommend.
- **Growth after launch.** [SEO and AI search work](/growth) continues after launch for teams that want it.

## CRM, analytics, and forms wired in during the build

A website is only useful once it talks to everything else, so we connect it while the build is under way and not after launch.

- CRM integration and marketing automation.
- GA4 setup and conversion tracking.
- Custom API integrations.
- Manual QA, cross-browser testing, and launch coordination.

## A team behind the site after launch

Your site keeps moving after launch without adding to your team. [Ongoing website support](/ongoing-website-support) includes:

- Weekly updates and initiatives, not quarterly releases.
- Quarterly conversion rate optimization (CRO) audits.
- Technical SEO monitoring of rankings, crawl errors, and technical health.
- Bug fixes and maintenance, with a priority SLA available for mission-critical sites.
- A dedicated pod and integration in your Slack, with no tickets required.

## When another option is better

This page is about the public marketing website of a B2B SaaS company. Another option is better in these cases:

- The site is one page, and a template or a page builder is enough.
- The work is the product interface, such as in-app screens or a logged-in dashboard.
- The positioning is undecided, and a new design cannot settle who the product is for.
- **Nobody on your team** will own the site after launch.
- **Engineering requires** the marketing site to live inside the product codebase.
