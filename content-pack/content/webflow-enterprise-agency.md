Enterprise website projects add security reviews, approval steps, several teams, and several languages to the usual design and build. BenorMedia, a Webflow Professional Partner, builds Webflow websites for B2B companies from growing teams to large enterprises, and stays on after launch with a dedicated team.

JOOR, a direct client for more than 3 years, is listed on our [work page](/work).

## Security and access questions answered up front

Enterprise reviews ask about access, data handling, and who can publish, and the Global & secure step of our process covers the parts your security and compliance teams ask about.

- **Access controls.** Access for the people who edit, review, and publish.
- **Security audits.** A security review as part of the build.
- **SSL implementation.** Secure connections set up for the site.
- **Stakeholder alignment.** The people who must approve are named before design starts.

## Global sites in more than one language

A multi-language CMS and regional content workflows let regional teams own their content on one site.

- Multi-language CMS architecture.
- Localization workflows for regional teams.
- Regional content workflows that fit your approval steps.

Webflow supports several locales through its [Localize add-on](https://webflow.com/feature/localize), and the number of locales depends on your plan, as of October 2026.

## Strategy first, then a component system

Most surprises in a build are questions nobody asked early, and on an enterprise project the early questions are about who approves what.

- A technical audit, content inventory, redirect mapping, and sitemap architecture before anything is built.
- Timeline planning and stakeholder alignment across the teams that sign off.
- A component library, so regional and brand teams build from pieces that already match.
- Variables and design tokens that carry your brand into every new layout.

Before design starts, name one approver per area and write the release path down.

## Integrations and analytics wired in during the build

Enterprise sites connect to more systems, so each integration is wired in during the build and not after launch.

- CRM integration and marketing automation.
- GA4 setup and conversion tracking.
- Custom API integrations.

## A dedicated team and response times after launch

[Ongoing website support](/ongoing-website-support) gives larger teams a dedicated pod, with a team lead, an integrated project manager, designers, and developers.

- SLA-backed response times, from a 48-hour standard response to 6-hour critical bug fixes, with a priority SLA available for mission-critical sites.
- Integration in your Slack, with no tickets required.
- Monthly check-ins and quarterly reviews.
- Full visibility into the work as it happens.

- **The website is mainly a product application** with logged-in features.
