- **S4. The six client-facing steps of the AEO program** (Sergio's own sales copy, no tool names). Use the step names and meaning as written; do not add promises.
  1. **Baseline & Audit.** A full technical read of the new site, plus where you currently appear, or don't, in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google AI Overviews. This is your "before" picture.
  2. **Keyword & Topic Mapping.** What your buyers actually search and ask, which topics competitors already own, and where the gaps are.
  3. **Technical AEO Implementation.** The AI-specific technical layer built into the site: structured data, entity definition and crawler access, none of which is standard in a website build.
  4. **Content Production.** Comparison pages, alternatives pages, FAQs and category content, written, optimized and published.
  5. **Ongoing Measurement.** Citations, rankings and AI visibility tracked on a fixed cadence and measured against the baseline, so movement is provable rather than claimed.
  6. **Monthly Roadmap.** Results feed the next month's prioritized plan, which you review and approve.
- **S5. Measurement method.** A fixed set of 30 to 50 buyer prompts (category, comparison, problem, alternatives, branded); a baseline across all five platforms before any work begins, with screenshots; presence rate measured across repeated queries, because AI answers are not deterministic; reporting every two weeks against the baseline, showing the change.
- **S6. AEO language rules.** Never say the technical step "fixes" anything (it is net-new work the build scope never included). Never name tools in client-facing material. Promise movement in citation presence, never revenue outcomes. Positioning line (internal, do not paste): monitoring tools show you are invisible; BenorMedia works inside your site and ships the changes.
- **S7. Off-site scope.** Owned-profile consistency and citable content are in scope. Active PR outreach is excluded or referred out.
- **S8. Audience.** Marketing leads (primary buyer) and founders at Series A to C B2B SaaS, fintech, AI, cybersecurity and HR-tech companies. US first, then UK, Germany, Denmark, Spain and Canada. Enterprise teams are a second audience.
- **S9. Measurement stance.** The plan promises visibility movement, not revenue.
