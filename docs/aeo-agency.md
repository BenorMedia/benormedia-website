### 4. What AEO and GEO actually involve.
> Component: the "Rebuild in Webflow with a component library." section (H2, intro, list, diagram).
> Visual: a simple diagram, "SEO foundations" as the base layer with four blocks on top: Prompt tracking, Answer-shaped pages, Third-party presence, Entity consistency.

- H2: What AEO and GEO actually involve.
- Intro: Answer engine optimization (AEO) and generative engine optimization (GEO) are two names for the same job: making your company the one AI systems mention and link to. Some agencies sell it as AI SEO or AI search optimization. Google says its AI features have [no special requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) beyond being indexed and eligible for a snippet. So the work stands on traditional SEO and adds four things most SEO programs skip.
- Items:
  - **Prompt tracking.** A fixed set of buyer questions, run across AI platforms on a schedule, because answers change from one run to the next. Prompts are longer and more specific than keywords, like "best SOC 2 automation tool for a 50-person fintech", and that's where buyers ready to shortlist show up.
  - **Answer-shaped pages.** Pages that give direct answers, with the numbers and the trade-offs in plain sentences AI models can quote.
  - **Third-party presence.** Brand mentions on the lists, review sites and communities AI systems cite when your own site isn't enough.
  - **Entity and knowledge graph optimization.** The same company name, description and category on your site, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, G2 and Wikidata where you qualify, tied together with Organization schema and sameAs links.
- Close: If your site runs on Webflow, our [Webflow SEO and AEO](/webflow-seo-agency) page covers the platform settings that matter.

### 5. How we run an AEO and GEO program.
> Component: the "Every page moved by hand" section (H2, intro, list). Six items; if the component caps at five, merge items 5 and 6.

- H2: How we run an AEO and GEO program.
- Intro: Our generative engine optimization services follow the same six steps every time. The first two set your AEO strategy, and they finish before we change anything on your site.
- Items:
  - **1. Prompt set.** 30 to 50 questions your buyers ask, built from sales calls, keyword research and search data: category, comparison, alternatives, problem and pricing prompts.
  - **2. Baseline.** How often each AI platform names you, which competitors it names instead, and which pages it cites as sources. We also review your wider digital presence: reviews, listings and mentions.
  - **3. Technical optimization.** robots.txt rules for search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Claude-SearchBot, content rendered on the server, and Bing Webmaster Tools with IndexNow for Copilot and Bing.
  - **4. Pages that answer.** New and rewritten pages for the prompts you lose: comparisons, alternatives, use cases, pricing, integrations and FAQs. We plan them in topic clusters, so the content strategy builds topical authority instead of one-off posts.
  - **5. Digital PR and mentions.** Outreach to the lists and publications AI already cites in your category, link building where it's earned, and review and community work.
  - **6. Reporting.** Mention rate by platform and prompt, cited pages, AI-referred visits and the pipeline they bring in, every month.

### 13. FAQ
- H2: AEO agency FAQs.

**Q: What is AEO?**
A: Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the work of getting your company mentioned and cited by AI answer engines: Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Microsoft Copilot. The term dates from the era of featured snippets and voice search; today it mostly means AI-generated answers. It uses the same foundations as SEO, plus prompt tracking, answer-first pages and third-party mentions.

**Q: What's the difference between AEO, GEO and SEO?**
A: Traditional SEO targets Google rankings and clicks from search results. AEO and GEO target mentions in AI answers. GEO, generative engine optimization, comes from a 2023 research paper; AEO is the name most marketers use, so a generative engine optimization agency and an AEO agency sell the same work. In practice it's one program, because AI answers draw on the same indexed pages your SEO strategy works on.

**Q: What is an AI SEO agency?**
A: Usually the same thing as an AEO or GEO agency: a team that works on your visibility in AI-generated answers as well as in search results. Some agencies use "AI SEO" to mean SEO done with AI tools instead, so ask which one you're buying.

**Q: How do we choose an AEO agency?**
A: The best answer engine optimization agencies show their method before the pitch. Ask to see the prompt set, which platforms they track and how often, and how they tell real changes from run-to-run noise. Ask what they'll change on your site and what they do off it, such as digital PR. Ask for work in your industry and a sample monthly report with mention rate, citations and pipeline. Then compare the monthly cost with your SEO budget, because most of the work overlaps. If an agency can't show these, keep looking.

**Q: Can you guarantee we'll appear in ChatGPT?**
A: No. ChatGPT, Google and the other platforms decide what they show, and their answers change between runs. We commit to the work, the measurement and a clear reason for every change. Treat any guarantee of AI placement as a warning sign.

**Q: How long does AEO take to show results?**
A: AI search engines that search the web live, such as Perplexity and ChatGPT search, can pick up a new or rewritten page soon after it's crawled. Moving your mention rate across a whole prompt set takes months of content and third-party work, so we report progress monthly from the start.

**Q: How do you measure AI visibility?**
A: We run a fixed set of 30 to 50 buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google's AI answers on a regular schedule. Each report shows the share of answers that name you, the pages they cite, and the visits and pipeline that come from AI referrals.

**Q: Do we need an llms.txt file?**
A: It doesn't hurt, and we add one, but don't expect it to change your visibility. Google says its Search ignores llms.txt, and a study of about 300,000 domains found no measurable effect on AI citations.

**Q: Does AEO work on Webflow and on custom-coded sites?**
A: Yes. The work is mostly content, structure and authority. On Webflow we use the native schema field, crawler settings and, on Team and Enterprise plans with the Analyze add-on, Webflow's own AEO analytics. On custom-coded sites we make the same changes in code.

**Q: How much do AEO and GEO services cost?**
A: Agencies usually charge a monthly retainer for answer engine optimization services, sometimes after a one-off audit. Ours is part of the Growth (SEO/GEO + CRO) plan, billed monthly and cancellable anytime; the [pricing page](/pricing) lists the price and what's included.

**Q: Can we do AEO in-house?**
A: Yes, if someone owns it. You'll need prompt tracking, a writer who can interview your experts, access to the website, and time for outreach. Some marketing teams keep the writing in-house or with a content marketing agency, and bring in an AEO specialist for the technical work and the outreach.
